# Ultramodern Full Micro Verticals Program

## Execution Notes

The completed single-preset MV program solved the platform base scope:

1. one public preset,
2. real router seams,
3. MF SSR and trust contracts,
4. Effect-first propagation contracts,
5. delivery-reference documentation.

That was necessary but not sufficient. This follow-up program covers the remaining downstream adoption work: how teams actually scaffold, extract, certify, and operate Micro Verticals without inventing bespoke process around the finished framework.

## Constraints

1. Keep this as a downstream adoption program, not a justification for more framework-core drift.
2. Preserve one public preset and explicit compatibility lanes.
3. Reuse completed docs, fixtures, and contract surfaces instead of creating parallel examples.
4. Keep the umbrella plan a coordination node; the detailed work belongs in child plans.

## Operator Guidance

Use this plan as the entrypoint for all work that comes after the finished framework base:

- starter and workspace shape,
- extraction and ownership governance,
- and production operations plus certification.

If new work cannot be mapped to one of those three lanes, it is probably either out of scope or a signal that the umbrella plan is trying to absorb unrelated product work.
